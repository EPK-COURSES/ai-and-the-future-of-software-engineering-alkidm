# Reflection on the future of software engineering jobs 

  During this assignment regarding the role of artificial intelligence in the future of programming-related jobs, the aspect which surprised me the most was the speed and accuracy that AI tools have in generating lines of code, which give the correct output. Also, the ability to identify errors in the written code in such a fast time is quite impressive. However, this development is also accompanied by some concerns, regarding the advancement of artificial intelligence. Junior developers, due to the aforementioned abilities that AI possesses, tend to copy code without understanding its logic, reducing the level of their critical thinking, as well as causing problems regarding the security of the system. Furthermore, another worrying factor, as a first-year Bachelor's student, is the higher expectations from employers on junior developers compared to a few years ago, due to the development of AI and its integration with classical programming. 
  Despite these challenges, artificial intelligence offers new opportunities for programmers to explore. In my opinion, as AI takes over the manual aspects of writing code and testing, software engineers have more opportunities to focus on solving complex problems and innovation. Critical thinking, system design, and interactive communication are fundamental skills that AI cannot replace. Also, requirements engineering, code review, and compliance with security standards are some of the areas that cannot be led by artificial intelligence. This requires engineers to adapt by becoming more of code managers and observers, rather than simply software developers.  
  This research has changed my approach to artificial intelligence. At first, I was skeptical about the use of AI, due to the negative impact it could have on programmers’ employment. However, now that I am more informed, this tool is simply replacing engineers who do not implement it in their workflow. Personally, to adapt to this development, I intend to create strong knowledge fundamentals regarding software architecture, data structures and techniques for integrating AI into programming. As a result, I would be able to plan the role of artificial intelligence and guide its activity, in order to be a software engineer updated with the new requirements.
