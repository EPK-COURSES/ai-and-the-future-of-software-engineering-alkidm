# Topic 7 - Future of Software Engineering Jobs

## 1. Introduction
      Nowadays, software engineering is evolving at a considerable pace. Programmers are no longer focusing only on the use of programming languages and frameworks, but
    also on the implementation of AI in various processes and activities related to this field. This use has occurred for various reasons such as the need of businesses
    to perform faster operations, the demand of users to receive the best services in the shortest possible time etc. This situation has caused a debate whether 
    traditional methods should be prioritised in this discipline or whether AI has more advantages in the economic development. In this assignment, several aspects 
    related to software engineering jobs will be addressed to identify and analyze the skills and strategies that programmers need, to be part of the technological market.

## 2. Changing Junior Development Roles

      The use of artificial intelligence applications, such as ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot is redimensioning the technology sector. A few years ago, junior developers
    focused on writing, debugging simple codes and learning frameworks. According to Pantin [1] , artificial intelligence is handling these repetitive operations,
    making the job of junior software engineers directed to validation of code and system integration.
      On the other hand, this situation is causing significant technical difficulties for programmers due to the dependence, artificial intelligence is creating.
    According to a study conducted at Lund University [2], this dependence on AI is reducing the level of problem-solving skills and code-logic.
    Therefore, these programmers must evolve from extreme users of artificial intelligence to engineers who combine this technology with strict architectural and
    security standards.
      Furthermore, this technological evolution has a direct effect on the employment of this category of programmers. According to Mårtensson &
    Westerholm [2] because of Large Language Models’ ability to generate code syntax more easily, companies are finding innovative ways to conduct interviews. Now,
    employers are testing junior candidates on high-level methods for solving a problem, system design and their level of knowledge about "AI-assisted development" 
    to analyse their skills in implementing these aspects.
    
      Conclusion: Due to the advantage that AI shows in generating manual parts of code, junior developers should focus more on the logical aspect of problem-solving and
    additional knowledge, regarding architectural patterns and AI assistance.
    
## 3. Future expectations from senior engineers

      According to the report "Software Engineering Insights 2025" [3], the modern development of software engineering is being significantly influenced by factors such as
    the collaboration of AI, cloud computing scalability or advanced DevOps practices. Companies are experiencing significant changes in their structural organization due
    to the decrease in the level of critical thinking of employees due to dependence on artificial intelligence, financial pressures from cloud expansion and the lack of
    clear boundaries regarding AI activity. The report also encourages companies to prioritize the development of staff knowledge and collaboration with universities and
    academic centers. Rather than replacing the human aspect, the automation requires balancing this innovation with sustainable practices.
      Considering the report "The Future of Software Engineering by 2050s: Will AI Replace Software Engineers?" [4]: projections predict that the field of software
    engineering will change from replacing complicated low-level layers for creating a software system, to more human-friendly ones in terms of software design. These
    technological developments will simplify the process of the software-related processes’ cycle, allowing users to generate the logic part related to the functioning of
    an application based on their requirements. However, long-term forecasts strongly reassure programmers that these automated systems will not replace the role of human
    developers. Instead, programmers will be a connecting bridge in this transition as a balancer of this development. As a result, this industry  will be able to use
    artificial intelligence as a complementary tool rather than an indicator in the creation of software systems independently of humans. This requires future engineers to
    constantly update their practical skills and keep searching constantly for new opportunities.
    
      Conclusion: According to predictions, AI will not replace programmers, but will serve as a tool for their job. However, continuous updating and informing regarding
    practical skills is a necessary element in this transition for software engineers.

    
## 4. Emerging AI-Related Engineering Roles 

     The widespread use and integration of AI, along with LLM, is drastically changing the job of a programmer. Based on the research by Sabina-Cristiana Necula (2023)
    and that by Treude & Gerosa (2025), the roles of software engineers are shifting from manual coding to analytical aspects.

     According to Necula [5], the market is moving towards more efficient data analysis and problem solving with a high degree of complexity: 
    • MLOps Specialists: a collaborative role between software engineering and data science, which focuses on implementing various artificial intelligence models. 
    • AI Integration Engineers: Creating a group specialized in integrating pre-built pieces of code generated by AI into complex software architectures.

     Treuda & Gerosa analyze in their study: "A Taxonomy of Human-AI Collaboration in Software Engineering" [6] , the new job positions that are “finding their position”
    due to the development of artificial intelligence: 
    • AI Code Evaluators: Specialists in the field of programming whose task is to control several parameters of the code generated by AI such as efficiency, security and architecture. 
    • Prompt / Context Engineers: Engineers who connect the financial aspect, regarding the specific rules that a business may have, with the technical aspect of programming. 
    • Agentic Architects: Engineers who integrate different AI "agent" systems with each other and monitor this collaboration.

     Conclusion: The new roles of programming, due to the evolution of artificial intelligence, require more critical thinking than before, due to the more specific
    logical, critical and architectural aspects that this technology imposes.

## 5. Automation of Repetitive Tasks 

     The automation of routine tasks by AI is becoming one of the most important factors in the change and development of software engineering. Tools like GitHub Copilot
    or LLM are creating a new modality for how teams of programmers are managing their work, regarding tasks such as: searching and writing efficient code syntax or
    generating previously used structures.

     According to a study conducted by GitHub [7], in the analysis conducted on the impact of artificial intelligence on task productivity, automation is a key factor in
    the efficiency of programmers' work: 
    • Reduction of the duration of tasks: This experiment shows that software engineers, who use AI more often, complete tasks 55.8% faster than those who continue to use classic methods. 
    • Reduction of workload: Since AI is generating repetitive parts of the code, engineers focus more on solving complex problems, which require more precise logic and architectural patterns.

     To better understand how this automation process works in practice, research by Barke, James, and Polikarpova [8] analyzes the interaction between programmers and
    code automation. In this research, they identified several collaborations between programmers and artificial intelligence: 
    • AI as an automation engine: The programmer uses AI as an acceleration modality in code generation. Meanwhile, artificial intelligence focuses on performing
    automated tasks for creating software by writing entire lines of code based on the requirements given by software engineers. 
    • Process evolution: The process is no longer performed solely by the human skills of programmers, but the mechanical work is left to AI. While processes that require
    more significant skills are performed by engineers.

     Conclusion: Automating manual tasks does not aim to replace programmers, but to shift their focus on more essential processes, such as high-level analysis and
    architectural patterns, due to the efficiency and productivity of this method by over 55 %.

## 6. Human skills which are difficult to automate 

     Although artificial intelligence is doing a pretty good job in generating lines of code quickly and automating routine tasks, its limitations in terms of structure 
    and logic, mean that software engineering is a discipline that operates parallel to other human disciplines. Recent researches in this field are showing that AI-based tools are not replacing programmers, but are shifting their work towards high-level "creative" and structural skills that machines are unable to do. 
 
     A. Requirements Clarity and the Role of Social Intelligence 
    One of the biggest obstacles regarding software engineering is related to requirements engineering and the way requirements are articulated by customers and managers: 
    1. According to Welsh's analysis [9], the role of engineers will be significantly transformed compared to previous years. The automation of code has led to the human aspect being focused on non-programming skills, such as management or interactive communication. 
    2. AI does not possess social skills and is unable to understand the context of a situation to reveal the real needs of stakeholders. This fact makes this ability remain entirely exclusive to humans.

     B. Choosing the architectural design and solving complex problems: 
    1. According to Ebert and Louridas [10], AI models do not possess critical thinking. The design of architectures requires several factors, such as: performance, scalability or economic plan. 
    2. The impact of integrating different modules in a company's infrastructure cannot be predicted by AI, because as a process it requires human experience and intuition.

    C. Security and Legal Issues: 
    1. Ebert and Louridas [10] argue that AI tools often generate correct code, but simply make logical simplifications that violate security standards. 
    2. Surveillance of AI’s agreement with security terms is a responsibility which totally belongs to software engineers. Machines cannot be responsible for ethical or security issues, leaving the legal liability on the project's programmers or managers.
    
     Conclusion: The automation of software engineering does not devalue the role of programmers in projects. Also, if the level of knowledge is at the required standard,
    it even elevates this profession to a higher level. According to studies, the skills that will identify successful engineers in the future are: critical thinking,
    communication and resource management.
