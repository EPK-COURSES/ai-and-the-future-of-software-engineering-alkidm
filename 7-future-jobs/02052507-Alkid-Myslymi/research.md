# Topic 7 - Future of Software Engineering Jobs

## 1. Introduction
      Nowadays, software engineering is evolving at a considerable pace. Programmers are no longer focusing only on the use of programming languages and frameworks, but
    also on the implementation of AI in various processes and activities related to this field. This use has occurred for various reasons such as the need of businesses
    to perform faster operations, the demand of users to receive the best services in the shortest possible time etc. This situation has caused a debate whether 
    traditional methods should be prioritised in this discipline or whether AI has more advantages in the economic development. In this assignment, several aspects 
    related to software engineering jobs will be addressed to identify and analyze the skills and strategies that programmers need, to be part of the technological market.

## 2. Changing Junior Development Roles

      The use of artificial intelligence applications, such as ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot is redimensioning the technology sector. A few years ago, junior developers
    focused on writing, debugging simple codes and learning frameworks. According to Pantin [2] , artificial intelligence is handling these repetitive operations,
    making the job of junior software engineers directed to validation of code and system integration.
      On the other hand, this situation is causing significant technical difficulties for programmers due to the dependence, artificial intelligence is creating.
    According to a study conducted at Lund University [1], this dependence on AI is reducing the level of problem-solving skills and code-logic.
    Therefore, these programmers must evolve from extreme users of artificial intelligence to engineers who combine this technology with strict architectural and
    security standards.
      Furthermore, this technological evolution has a direct effect on the employment of this category of programmers. According to Mårtensson &
    Westerholm [1] because of Large Language Models’ ability to generate code syntax more easily, companies are finding innovative ways to conduct interviews. Now,
    employers are testing junior candidates on high-level methods for solving a problem, system design and their level of knowledge about "AI-assisted development" 
    to analyse their skills in implementing these aspects.
    
## 3. Future expectations from senior engineers

      According to the report "Software Engineering Insights 2025" [3], the modern development of software engineering is being significantly influenced by factors such as
    the collaboration of AI, cloud computing scalability or advanced DevOps practices. Companies are experiencing significant changes in their structural organization due
    to the decrease in the level of critical thinking of employees due to dependence on artificial intelligence, financial pressures from cloud expansion and the lack of
    clear boundaries regarding AI activity. The report also encourages companies to prioritize the development of staff knowledge and collaboration with universities and
    academic centers. Rather than replacing the human aspect, the automation requires balancing this innovation with sustainable practices.
      Considering the report "The Future of Software Engineering by 2050s: Will AI Replace Software Engineers?" [4]: projections predict that the field of software
    engineering will change from replacing complicated low-level layers for creating a software system, to more human-friendly ones in terms of software design. These
    technological developments will simplify the process of the software-related processes’ cycle, allowing users to generate the logic part related to the functioning of
    an application based on their requirements. However, long-term forecasts strongly reassure programmers that these automated systems will not replace the role of human
    developers. Instead, programmers will be a connecting bridge in this transition as a balancer of this development. As a result, this industry  will be able to use
    artificial intelligence as a complementary tool rather than an indicator in the creation of software systems independently of humans. This requires future engineers to
    constantly update their practical skills and keep searching constantly for new opportunities.
